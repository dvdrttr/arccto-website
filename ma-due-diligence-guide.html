5 Hidden Tech Costs That Tank Small Business Buyouts

When you buy a business, financial balance sheets don't show tech rot. Backed by 26 years of real-world experience, here is the exact plain-English checklist we use to find hidden software costs, subscription traps, and hardware risks before you sign the closing papers.

01 // The "Ghost App" & Auto-Renewing Contract Trap

Many small businesses pay thousands monthly for software subscriptions no one uses. Watch out for multi-year vendor contracts with aggressive auto-renewals that transfer to you the second you buy the place.

02 // Custom Workarounds & "Solo IT Guy" Hostage Situations

If the business runs on a custom database built by a single contractor who left three years ago, you are buying a ticking time bomb. Make sure you actually own the code and know how it works.

03 // Held-Hostage Passwords & Domain Ownership

Make sure the target company actually owns its website domain and master admin accounts—and not some outsourced IT agency refusing to hand over the keys.

04 // Fake Backups & Ransomware Risks

Having a backup isn't the same as actually being able to restore your business. We check if your data is truly safe off-site and test exactly how long it takes to get back up and running.

05 // Sloppy Customer Data Handling

Check exactly where customer credit cards and personal records live on the network. Storing this stuff sloppily can trigger massive fines the moment you take over.

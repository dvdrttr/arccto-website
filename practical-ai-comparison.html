01 // Microsoft 365 Copilot: Best for Excel & Heavy Outlook Inboxes

If your business lives in spreadsheets and formal Word proposals, Microsoft 365 Copilot excels at pulling financial data across files and generating instant meeting summaries inside Teams.

02 // Google Workspace Gemini: Best for Collaboration & Speed

If your team collaborates natively in Google Docs, Sheets, and Drive, Gemini is faster at sorting incoming customer inquiries and summarizing scattered project folders into actionable briefs.

Both tools keep your company data inside your private tenant when configured properly. Neither Microsoft nor Google trains public AI models on your proprietary business files if licensing is set correctly.
